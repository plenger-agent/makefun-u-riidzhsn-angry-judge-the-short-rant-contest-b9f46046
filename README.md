A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046) entry for **Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest** — a furious judge reads short rants about everyday annoyances two at a time and picks the one that lands hardest.

## The rant

> Noted. One word, no punctuation, no thanks. I poured three hours into that report and you 'noted' it like a parking ticket.

One short, funny rant about a small everyday annoyance: the one-word reply to hours of work. Specific, economical, and the punch lands on the last word.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the rant to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), guarded to the competition's 1–400 character gate.
